You've totally got this

Your Sunny Isles Homecoming

15901 Collins Ave · about $2.25 and two easy buses away

Here's the honest shape of this trip: one free train, two buses, one pretty walk. Buses come every 10–30 minutes, so nothing here is missable — the worst case is waiting with an ocean breeze. Every step below has a real Street View button so you can peek at the actual corner before you're standing on it. Heads up: two stops were listed differently in an older version of this plan (37th & 38th). The verified stops are 40th St and Collins & 43rd — the buttons below point to the right corners.
Money — pick one, then forget about it
$2.25
EASY Ticket from the machine at the train station (Step 3). Tap it on both buses — the second one's free.
$4.50
Apple Pay, if the machine line is long. Totally fine — just use the same phone on both buses.
3
The free skytrain

MIA Mover → Airport Station

Free5 minGrab ticket here
📸 Tap to see the Mover station
What you'll seeBig overhead signs reading MIA Mover / Rental Car Center on Level 3 — two escalators up from baggage claim. The train is automated, glassy, and there's only one place it goes. Impossible to take it the wrong way.
  • Ride to the end — that's Miami Airport Station.
  • Find the EASY Ticket machine in the station and load one $2.25 fare (takes 2 minutes, saves $2.25).
  • Then head down to the ground-level bus bays.
♡
Pro safety tipTurn on live location sharing before you get on the Mover — signal dips inside. And keep your suitcase handle in your hand on the train, not parked across the aisle.
4
Bus one · the express

Route 150 over the bay

25 minEvery 30 minAim for 12:08
📸 Tap to see the bus bays
What you'll seeA row of numbered bus bays at street level. Yours is the one signed 150 — the bus itself reads 150 MIAMI BEACH on the front display. Departures: 11:38, 12:08, 12:38, 1:08.
  • Tap your EASY Ticket (or phone) flat on the farebox by the driver.
  • Sit near the front, bag between your feet — the ride crosses a gorgeous long bridge over Biscayne Bay. That bridge = you're going the right way.
  • Your stop is Indian Creek Dr & 40 St, about 25 minutes in. It's a scheduled timepoint — the bus stops there every trip.
If timing slipsMissed the 12:08? The 12:38 is the exact same bus, 30 minutes later. Zero sprinting required — ever.
♡
Pro safety tipSet a get-off alert for "Indian Creek Dr & 40 St" in the Transit app, then you can look at the water instead of the route map.

What you'll seeStreet numbers climbing — 41, 42, 43 — which means you're headed the right way. 41st St is the big bright corner with shops; that's your halfway landmark, not a wrong turn. Your stop is on the ocean side of Collins at 43rd, where the northbound buses pull in.
Permission to bailIf the walk with a suitcase in the heat feels like enough adventure for one day — 41st & Collins is a perfect rideshare pickup. Big, bright, easy for a driver to find, ~$30–40 home, zero guilt. You still did the cheap part.
♡
Pro safety tipWheeled bag behind you, crossbody in front, phone in hand. Cross Collins only at the signals — it's wide and drivers here are… Miami drivers.

What you'll seeBuses roll in every few minutes. Glance at the glowing front display: AVENTURA MALL = your ride. Haulover = wave it past like a pro. Not sure? "Does this go to 159th Street?" — drivers get asked constantly.
  • Tap the same ticket or same phone as bus one. EASY Ticket riders: this tap is free.
  • Settle in — Bal Harbour's designer windows, then a long stretch of green park, then the towers return.
If the wrong bus happens anywayGenuinely fine: stay on to Bal Harbour Shops, hop off somewhere bright and staffed, and catch the next Aventura-signed 100 or grab a 10-minute car. Just don't get off in the park stretch itself.
♡
Pro safety tipBag in front of your knees, one earbud out so you catch announcements, get-off alert set for 159 St. Then the last 20 minutes are officially scenery time.
